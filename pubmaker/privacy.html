Privacy Policy: PubMaker: Pub File Editor & Desktop Publishing

Applies to PubMaker for Windows, all versions, including its command-line tool. Developed and maintained by Dhinovatech, www.dhinovatech.com.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

The short version. PubMaker collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it does not send your documents, pictures, mail-merge lists or anything else to us or to anyone else. We, the developer, receive no data from this app at all, and we have no server that could receive it.

PubMaker never connects to the internet. Its app package does not declare the internet capability, so Windows does not let it. Your documents leave your PC only when you export, print, email or share them yourself.

1. Your documents

Everything you make in PubMaker (flyers, newsletters, cards, calendars and the rest) is saved where you choose. New documents go to Documents\PubMaker unless you pick another folder. They are ordinary files on your PC, and only you decide where copies of them go.

PubMaker does not upload, sync or back up your documents anywhere. Its templates, artwork and colour schemes are built into the app, so using them involves no download.

2. What PubMaker keeps on your PC

To work, PubMaker keeps a few files of its own in its app data folder on your PC (%LOCALAPPDATA%\PubMaker, which Windows keeps inside PubMaker's own package data):

  • settings.json: your preferences, such as language, paper size, units and any reminders you set
  • viewstate.json: the page, zoom and selection you last had in each document, so it reopens where you left off
  • library.db: a local search index of the PubMaker documents in your library folders, holding their file paths, names, titles, dates, sizes, page counts, small thumbnails, their text (so you can search inside them), and your favourites, pins and recently opened list
  • Recovery\: a recovery copy of open documents, so your work survives a crash or power cut
  • Snapshots\: earlier versions of your documents, so you can go back to them
  • Templates\: templates you save yourself
  • Logs\: a plain-text log of app events and errors (section 10)

All of this is produced on your PC, by code running on your PC, and stays on your PC. None of it is sent anywhere. Windows removes this folder when you uninstall PubMaker. Your documents in your own folders are not touched.

3. No internet connection

PubMaker makes no network connections of any kind. There is no telemetry, no crash reporting service, no usage analytics, no advertising code, no update check of its own and no sign-in. Updates come through the Microsoft Store, like any other Store app.

This is enforced by Windows, not only promised: the PubMaker package does not declare the internetClient capability, so Windows does not give it network access.

A few buttons hand something over to another app on your PC: Rate PubMaker opens the Microsoft Store, Send feedback opens your email app, and a link you add to a document opens in your browser when you click it. Whatever those apps do is under their own privacy terms. PubMaker itself sends nothing.

4. Opening .pub and other files

When you open a Publisher (.pub) file, PubMaker reads it in a separate, locked-down helper process on your PC, with time and memory limits, so a damaged or hostile file cannot harm the app or your other documents. The file is converted on your PC and is never sent anywhere to be converted. The same is true of PDFs, pictures, SVG, Word, RTF, text and spreadsheet files you import.

PubMaker never writes .pub files, so your original Publisher files are left exactly as they were.

5. Mail merge

Names, addresses and other details you merge stay in your own spreadsheet or CSV file. PubMaker reads that file when you merge and keeps no separate copy of it. A list you type directly into PubMaker is saved inside that document and nowhere else.

6. Exports, prints and the free label

Exported PDFs, pictures and Word files carry only what you put in them, plus the document title and "PubMaker" with its version number as the creating app. PubMaker adds no hidden identifier, tracking code or information about you or your PC.

In the free version, each exported or printed page carries a small printed label, "PubMaker · dhinovatech.com". It is plain text on the page, the same for everyone, and it reports nothing to us. Pro removes it (section 7).

7. Buying Pro, volume licences and rating

Pro and the 24-hour pass. Every feature of PubMaker is free. The optional Pro add-on and the 24-hour pass remove the label, and both are sold through the Microsoft Store. The Store, not PubMaker, handles the payment, and Microsoft's privacy statement (privacy.microsoft.com) covers it. We never see your payment details, your name or your Microsoft account. PubMaker only asks Windows whether this PC has Pro, and Windows answers from the Store licence it keeps.

Volume licences. Schools and companies can use a signed .pubmaker-license file instead. PubMaker checks its signature on your PC with a key built into the app. There is no licence server, and nothing is sent when it is checked.

Rating. PubMaker may occasionally ask whether you would like to rate it. Rate PubMaker opens the Microsoft Store's own rating window, and anything you write there goes to Microsoft under their terms, not to the app. PubMaker remembers on your PC only when it last asked and whether you rated, so it does not keep asking.

8. Sending feedback

Send feedback opens a new email in your own email app, addressed to Dhinovatech support. It is filled in with your message, the PubMaker version, the Windows version and, if you are reporting a problem, a short description of it. You can read and change all of it before sending. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself, and you choose whether to attach anything.

If you do email us, we use your message and email address only to reply to you.

9. Reminders

If you set a reminder to make the next edition of a document, PubMaker shows a Windows notification at the time you chose. The reminder and the document it opens are saved in your settings on your PC. The notification is made on your PC and sent to no one. Reminders are off unless you add one.

10. App log

PubMaker writes app events and errors to a plain-text file in the Logs folder on your PC. The log never holds document content. It is never sent anywhere. It is yours to open, attach to an email to us if you choose, or delete.

11. Permissions PubMaker asks for

  • Full trust (runFullTrust): PubMaker is a desktop app. It needs this to print, to open files you choose anywhere on your PC, to run the separate helper process that reads .pub files, to open .pub and .pubmaker files when you double-click them, and to provide the pubmaker command-line tool. It does not need or ask for administrator rights.

PubMaker does not ask for internet access, your location, camera, microphone, contacts or account details.

12. Your control over your data

  • Your documents: they are ordinary files in your folders. Move, copy or delete them as you would any other file.
  • The library: remove a folder from your library to take its documents out of the search index. The documents themselves are not touched.
  • Reminders: turn any reminder off or remove it.
  • Delete everything: uninstall PubMaker. Windows removes its app data folder, including the settings, library index, recovery copies, snapshots, saved templates and log. Your own documents are not touched, because they are yours.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete for you and no account for you to close.

13. Children

PubMaker is a general-purpose desktop publishing tool. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them, or from anyone else.

14. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your data, because we never have it. The app contains no third-party advertising, tracking or profiling code.

15. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and the "Last updated" date above will change. If PubMaker ever needed the internet, that would be a change to this policy, and it would also be described in the app's release notes.
