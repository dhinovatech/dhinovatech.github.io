Your filing system did not fail. Life happened.

A Downloads folder with four thousand things in it. A Pictures folder holding three phones, two cameras and a decade of screenshots. Folders called “Holiday 2019”, “Copy of holiday” and “holiday (final)”, and no way to tell which is which.

Doing it by hand works, but nobody finishes. Cloud services will do it for you — by uploading your entire personal library to somebody else’s computer. Scriptable tools do exactly what you tell them, if you are willing to learn a rule syntax and test it on a copy first. And all of them, at the moment of truth, ask you to trust that the operation about to run across ten thousand files is the one you meant.

Stow puts an end to cluttered Downloads, sprawling photo libraries, and mystery folders. Powered by on-device vision, local document OCR, and CPU language models, Stow calculates intelligent organization plans, hunts down duplicate and blurry files, and sweeps away hidden junk.

Everything runs strictly on your PC with zero cloud servers, zero telemetry, and zero background tracking. Nothing ever moves without an approved preview, nothing is ever permanently deleted, and any operation can be reversed in a single click.

Guaranteed by Architecture

The Five Invariants

Five non-negotiable architectural guarantees built into every layer of Stow.

Invariant 1

100% Offline & Private

Zero accounts, zero cloud servers, zero telemetry, zero analytics, zero background tracking. All AI models (vision, OCR, language) run locally on your PC's CPU.

Invariant 2

Nothing Moves Without an Approved Preview

Stow never moves, renames, or cleans a file behind your back. Every single operation produces an itemized preview table you review and approve first.

Invariant 3

Nothing Is Ever Permanently Deleted

The app never executes permanent file deletions. Cleaning and duplicate removal operations move items to the familiar Windows Recycle Bin where they can easily be recovered.

Invariant 4

Instant One-Click Undo

Everything is journaled before disk execution. If you change your mind an hour or a week later, one click reverts your files to their exact previous locations.

Invariant 5

Safe with Cloud Folders

Automatically recognizes cloud placeholders (OneDrive, Dropbox, iCloud) and refuses to silently download gigabytes over metered connections.

Safe Execution Flow

How It Works

Three simple, transparent steps. Absolute control from start to finish.

1

Scan & Describe

Point Stow at any folder or drive. Select a 1-click preset, describe your desired arrangement in plain English, or let Stow automatically discover your existing filing conventions.

2

Preview & Verify

Review the complete proposal table before a single byte moves on disk. Every file has an exact row, proposed path, and governing rule. Filter, edit, or untick any item you prefer to leave untouched.

3

Apply with 1-Click Undo

Execute the plan with total confidence. Stow writes every operation to an atomic transaction journal first. If you ever change your mind—an hour or a month later—one click reverts everything.

Four models, none of them shipped inside the installer

Each is downloaded on first use of the feature that needs it, only after you have been shown the size and agreed, and each is verified against a SHA-256 hash pinned in the source. Every download URL is pinned to an immutable upstream commit, so the bytes fetched are always the bytes the hashes were taken from.

Declining leaves a fully working product: the presets, the manual builder, the convention learner, search, the space report, labels and duplicate detection need no models at all. Inference runs on the CPU via ONNX Runtime — no NPU or GPU provider is registered, and the application does not use the AI models built into Windows.

What Stow is not

  • Not a cloud photo service. It will not back anything up, sync anything between machines, or put a copy of your library anywhere but your own disk.
  • Not a duplicate remover that empties itself. Nothing is destroyed; the most it will ever do is put spare copies in the Recycle Bin at your request.
  • Not an automation daemon. It will notice files arriving in a watched folder, but it will not file them while you are not looking.
  • Not a black box. Every proposal comes with the rule that produced it, every rejection with an explanation, and the entire plan can be read in a spreadsheet before a single file moves.

Who it is for

The person with a Downloads folder that has become a landfill and a Pictures folder holding a decade of three different phones. The person who set up a filing system in 2019, kept it beautifully for four months, and would like the last five years put away the same way. The photographer who needs RAW and JPEG to stay together and needs to find the near-duplicates without uploading forty thousand images to anybody. And, running through all of them, the person who is not willing to send their personal files to a company’s servers in order to tidy them up.

Honest limits

Inference is CPU-only, so the first indexing pass over a large library is slow, and it is deliberately throttled further while running in the background. Model output can be wrong: a misread instruction produces an incorrect plan, which is precisely why the preview and the undo journal exist and why nothing is ever applied unattended.
