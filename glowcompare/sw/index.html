Clinical Methodology

Built For Data-Driven Skincare

Six powerful architectural pillars designed to eliminate product trial frustration and safeguard your skin barrier.

100% Local-First Privacy

Your private photos, routine records, and skin notes stay encrypted in your Android device's local SQLite sandbox. Zero cloud harvesting, zero central photo servers. Optional multi-device backup uses isolated Google Drive drive.appdata space.

Gemini Enterprise AI Diagnostics

Powered by Google's Enterprise Paid Tier API. Evaluates 7 core skin health metrics (Hydration, Redness, Texture, Clarity, Glow, Blemish, Elasticity). Under enterprise terms, photos are processed ephemerally with zero model training logging.

Visual Comparator Engine

Inspect changes via sub-pixel split sliders, 2-to-4 photo synchronized zoom grids, and automated facial landmarks alignment. Export smooth HD MP4 time-lapse animations detailing your skin's transformation.

Smart Routine Layering Stack

Automatically sorts your products by chemical molecular weights and vehicle thickness: Cleanser ➔ Toner/Essence ➔ Serums ➔ Eye Cream ➔ Moisturizer ➔ Sunscreen (AM) to maximize active ingredient absorption.

Active Chemical Conflict Protection

Prevents skin barrier disruption and chemical burns. Instantly alerts you to incompatible active combinations (Retinol + AHA/BHA, Vitamin C + Retinol, Benzoyl Peroxide + Tretinoin, Niacinamide + Direct Acids).

PAO Expiration & Shelf Inventory

Track Period-After-Opening (PAO) limits (3M, 6M, 12M, 24M) and start/stop dates. Receive proactive notifications before formulas degrade or harbor bacteria, and map milestones directly to skin flare-ups.
