Steptell Privacy Policy

Applies to Steptell for Windows, published by Dhinovatech on the Microsoft Store.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Steptell collects nothing. It has no server, no account, no analytics and no telemetry, and it does not connect to the internet. Everything it records stays on your PC unless you export or share a file yourself.

1. What Steptell accesses on your PC, and why

  • Mouse clicks and keyboard shortcuts, only while you record. Recording starts only when you press the hotkey or a record button, and a coloured border and tray icon show it is on. There is no hidden or background mode.
  • Keystrokes are never recorded. Steptell drops every character key the moment it is pressed, in memory. By default steps say only "Type in the Name box". If you turn on Include what I type (off by default), a step can show short text you typed, read from the field after you finish, such as a search term. Passwords, excluded apps, private windows and anything that looks like personal information are never included, and your organisation can turn the option off.
  • Screenshots of the screen, window or area you chose, one per step.
  • Names of buttons, menus and windows through Windows UI Automation, to write each step.
  • Text in screenshots, read on your PC with Windows' built-in text recognition, to find personal information (email addresses, phone numbers, card and bank numbers, IP addresses, ID numbers) so you can blur it, and to let you search your guides. This text stays in your encrypted library; it is never included in files you export.
  • Basic system details for problem reports: Windows version, screens, display language and time zone.
  • Windows diagnostics, only for a problem report. When you review a problem report, Steptell reads error entries from the Windows Application and System logs around the time you recorded (time, source, event number and level only; the event messages, which can contain names and file paths, are never read), plus memory, free disk space, time since restart, the kinds of network connection that are on and the power state. Everything is listed on the review screen, and you can leave it out of the report with one checkbox.
  • Windows voices, only when you export a narrated video. The speech is made on your PC with the voices installed in Windows.
  • The clipboard, only when you paste into Steptell or copy from it.
  • Files and folders you choose when you import, export or back up.

2. What Steptell never does

  • It never sends your guides, screenshots, steps or any usage data to Dhinovatech or anyone else.
  • It never records apps on its excluded list (password managers by default) or, on a best-effort basis, private and incognito browser windows.
  • It never shows ads and never asks you to sign in.

3. Where your data is stored

Your guides are stored in Steptell's own app folder in your Windows user profile, encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key that Windows protects for your user account. Deleted guides stay in Steptell's trash for 30 days and are then removed. Uninstalling Steptell deletes this folder. Files you exported or saved to folders you chose stay where you put them.

4. Purchases

Steptell Pro is bought through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles the payment and tells Steptell, through Windows, whether you own Pro or a 24-hour pass. Dhinovatech never receives your payment details. To check when a 24-hour pass ends, Steptell keeps on your PC the latest date and time it has seen, so setting the clock back does not extend a pass. Microsoft's privacy statement applies to the Store: privacy.microsoft.com.

5. Ratings and recommendations

Like it? Rate it opens the Microsoft Store's own rating dialog; your rating and review go to Microsoft, not to Steptell. Refer a friend opens the Windows share sheet with a short message and a link to Steptell's Store page; you choose who receives it, and Steptell sees neither the app you use nor the recipient. After a few exports Steptell may ask for a rating, at most once a month; Don't ask again stops it, and you can turn it back on in Settings.

6. Crash reports

If Steptell crashes, Windows Error Reporting may send a crash report to Microsoft according to your Windows diagnostic settings. Dhinovatech can see summary crash data for Steptell in Microsoft Partner Center. Steptell itself sends nothing.

7. Organisations

If your organisation manages Steptell, it may set policies (for example a mandatory privacy check or a fixed export folder) through Windows. These are read locally from the Windows registry. Organisation licence files are checked on your PC.

8. Children

Steptell does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes and contact
