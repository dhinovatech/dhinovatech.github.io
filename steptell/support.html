1. Getting started

  1. Open Steptell and accept the short privacy screen.
  2. Press Ctrl + Alt + Shift + F9 (or New recording), do the task, then press the hotkey again.
  3. Edit the steps, then choose Export to save Word, PDF, HTML, PowerPoint and more.

To send a problem to IT, choose Report a problem, show what goes wrong, stop, and save the ZIP file.

2. Frequently asked questions

Does Steptell record what I type?

No. Character keys are dropped the moment they are pressed, and steps say "Type in the Name box" without the text. Shortcuts such as Ctrl+S are recorded because they are part of the instructions. If you want steps such as Type "printer" in the Search box, turn on Settings > Privacy > Include what I type: Steptell then reads the short text from the field after you finish typing. Passwords and anything that looks like personal information are never included.

Does Steptell send anything over the internet?

No. Steptell has no server, account or telemetry and works fully offline. Only files you export or share leave your PC.

Can I open my old Steps Recorder files?

Yes. Drag a Steps Recorder ZIP or MHT file onto Steptell, or choose Add > Open or import files. You get an editable guide with every screenshot.

Why do some steps say "Click here"?

Some apps do not tell Windows the names of their buttons. Steptell marks the click on the screenshot instead. You can edit the step text at any time.

How does Steptell describe Windows itself?

In plain words, the way you would explain it: "Open the Start menu", "Click the search box in the Start menu", "Open Quick Settings", "Turn on Bluetooth in Settings", "Go to Documents in File Explorer", "Select 20 in the calendar". Windows shortcuts say what they do, for example "Press Windows + E to open File Explorer". This works on Windows 10 and Windows 11, in every language Steptell supports.

A step says the app runs as administrator.

Windows does not let normal apps read the buttons of apps that run as administrator. Steptell still takes the screenshot. To get full step text, choose Restart as administrator when Steptell offers it (your organisation may turn this off).

How do I hide passwords and personal information?

Password boxes are blurred automatically. Steptell also finds email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers and similar items in screenshots; the privacy check before export lists them with a one-click Blur all. You can blur, pixelate or cover any area yourself in the editor.

Can I move the "Created with Steptell" label?

Yes. In the export dialog (or Settings > Branding) choose Label position: top or bottom, left, centre or right. Automatic puts it in the bottom corner. The label always moves aside if it would cover the click or your notes.

What does Steptell Pro do?

Every feature is free. Free exports show a small "Created with Steptell by dhinovatech.com" label on each image. Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the label or replaces it with your own logo.

Need it for one job? A 24-hour pass removes the label from everything you export for 24 hours. Files you export during the pass stay label-free. You can buy Pro for life at any time.

I bought Pro but still see the label.

Open Settings > Steptell Pro > Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the Microsoft Store with the account you bought it with.

My narrated video has no voice.

Narration uses the voices installed in Windows. The export dialog says which chosen languages have no voice; those videos get captions only. Add a voice in Windows Settings > Time & language > Speech, then export again.

Can I add steps to a guide I already made?

Yes. In the editor choose Insert > Record steps here (Ctrl + Shift + A). When you stop recording, the new steps go in after the selected step.

How many screenshots can a recording have?

25 by default, like Steps Recorder. Change it in Settings > Recording to any number up to 999, or no limit.

How do I move my guides to another PC?

Use Settings > Library > Back up to create one password-protected file, then Restore from backup on the other PC. Single guides can be exported as .steptell files.

What happens when I uninstall Steptell?

Windows deletes Steptell's library, including your guides. Files you exported stay. Back up first if you want to keep your guides.

Can my company deploy and configure Steptell?

Yes: through Intune or Company Portal, with Group Policy/ADMX settings and organisation licences. Ask Dhinovatech for the IT kit.

3. Keyboard shortcuts

  • Start or stop recording: Ctrl + Alt + Shift + F9
  • Pause or resume, add a comment, capture now: see Settings > Hotkeys (all can be changed).
  • In the editor: Ctrl + Z undo, Ctrl + Y redo, Ctrl + F find and replace, Ctrl + E export, Ctrl + P print, F5 slideshow, Ctrl + Shift + P all commands with their shortcuts.
  • On a screenshot: press a tool's letter (A arrow, R rectangle, B blur…), then Enter to place it; arrow keys move it, Ctrl + arrow keys resize it, Tab moves between shapes, Delete removes one.
