Switching from Google Photos

Move your Google Photos library to your PC in three steps

Your dates, places, albums and favorites come with it. Nothing is uploaded at any point.

1

Export from Google

Use Google Takeout to download your Google Photos library as ZIP files. There is no need to unpack them.

2

Choose the ZIPs in Smart Photos

Smart Photos unpacks them onto your PC and keeps dates, places, descriptions, favorites and albums. The names you gave people come back as suggestions.

3

Check, then decide

"Is everything here?" compares the export with your library, so you know nothing was missed before you delete anything from the cloud.

Coming from Picasa, Lightroom, digiKam or Windows Photo Gallery? Smart Photos reads the face tags, stars and albums they saved with your photos, and your files stay unchanged. A download of your iCloud Photos comes across the same way as Google Takeout.

Privacy Policy

On-device AI, explained plainly

No account, no ads, no tracking, no telemetry. The AI models download once when you choose (about 1.8 GB); after that everything works offline, and Offline mode blocks the internet entirely. Every connection the app makes is listed in the app.

AI disclosure: Smart Photos uses on-device AI models to recognize what is in photos, group faces, read text and understand searches. When a search is not understood directly, an on-device language model rewrites it as a short search, shown as filters you can remove. It does not create images or content, and no AI runs in the cloud.

What you need

  • Windows 10 version 2004 or later, or Windows 11
  • An x64 or ARM64 PC
  • About 1.8 GB of free space for the AI models, downloaded once
  • Available in 25 languages

What Smart Photos is not

  • Not a cloud backup. It keeps your photos where they already are, so keep a copy on another drive as you would with any local library.
  • Not a photo editor. It opens your photos in the editors you already use.
  • Not a phone-to-PC sync service. It imports from a phone, camera or memory card when you connect one.
  • Not for Mac, iPhone or Android. Smart Photos is a Windows app.

Who it is for

People leaving Google Photos or iCloud Photos who want the same smart gallery without a monthly storage bill. Families with years of photos on a PC or an external drive. Photographers who shoot RAW and keep their own folders. Anyone who would rather their family's faces were recognized on their own computer, not in someone else's data center.
