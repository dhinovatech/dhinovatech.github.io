DECISION TOOLS

12 answers for the questions a loan raises

Rent or buy, refinance, prepay or invest, what you can afford and more.

How much can I borrow?

The loan a monthly payment can repay.

How long will it take?

How long a monthly payment takes to clear a loan.

What rate is this quote?

The real rate hidden in a quoted payment. Useful when a lender quotes an EMI but not a clear rate.

Debt-free by a date

What to pay extra each month to finish by your target.

What can I afford?

How much loan your income can carry, using the share of income lenders let debt take (DTI or FOIR).

What if rates rise?

Your payment if the rate goes up, so you can see what you could absorb.

Should I refinance?

Compares the rest of your loan with a new one, including costs. A lower payment from a longer term can still cost more overall.

Prepay or invest?

Puts the same money against the loan or into an investment, and compares wealth when the original loan would have ended.

Rent or buy?

Compares your net worth buying with renting and investing the difference, so both sides spend the same money.

How much down payment?

What each size of down payment does to your EMI and interest, and how long it takes to save.

What if I sell?

What you'd walk away with if you sold in a given year.

How often should I pay?

The same loan at each payment frequency.
