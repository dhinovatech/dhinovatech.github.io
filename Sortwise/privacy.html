Privacy Policy: Sortwise: Photo & File Organizer

Applies to Sortwise: Photo & File Organizer for Windows, all versions. Developed and maintained by Dhinovatech, www.dhinovatech.com.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The short version. Sortwise runs on your PC. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It does not send your files, photos, file names, searches, face data or locations to us or to anyone else. We, the developer, receive no data from this app at all, and we have no server that could receive it.

Sortwise works without the internet. Apart from Windows asking the Microsoft Store whether this PC has Pro, it goes online in only three cases, each one started by you: downloading an on-device pack, looking up a place name (off by default), and buying Pro. Sections 3 and 6 describe them.

1. What Sortwise does with your files

Sortwise reads the folders you give it ("places") and keeps a private index so it can sort, rename, find copies and search your files. Depending on the features you use, the index can hold:

  • File names, paths, sizes, types and dates
  • Photo details (EXIF), such as the camera, the date taken and any GPS location saved in the photo, and music tags such as artist and album
  • Thumbnails and small previews
  • Text read from documents and PDFs, if you turn on Read the text inside documents
  • Image "fingerprints" (embeddings), numbers used to search photos by what is in them and to spot look-alikes
  • Content hashes, used to find identical files
  • Faces found in photos, small face crops and face templates, if you turn on Find the people in photos, plus the names you give people
  • Your rules and tags, and a history of the changes Sortwise made, so they can be undone
  • How many file changes you have used this month on the free plan

All of this is produced on your PC, by code running on your PC, and stays on your PC.

2. Where that data is stored

Everything Sortwise creates is kept on your own machine, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Sortwise:

  • index.db: one local SQLite database holding everything listed in section 1
  • facecache\: small face crops, if you turned on finding people
  • models\: the on-device packs, if you downloaded any
  • logs\: a plain-text problem log
  • health_history.json: a small file of counts, such as how many copies were found over time

This folder is deliberately outside cloud-synced folders such as OneDrive, so Windows backup and file sync do not copy your index, including face data, to the cloud.

Nothing in this folder is sent anywhere. Sortwise has no cloud backup, no sync and no remote copy of your index. (Its own Backup feature copies your files only to a folder or drive you choose.)

3. When Sortwise uses the internet

3.1 Downloading on-device packs (optional, once each)

Three features need model files that are not part of the app:

  • Photo understanding: searching photos by what is in them, and spotting look-alikes
  • Faces: finding and grouping the people in your photos
  • Language: turning a description in your own words into rules

The first time you use one of these, Sortwise tells you how big the pack is and asks before downloading it. The files come from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), and each one is checked against a fixed SHA-256 checksum. A file that does not match is thrown away.

This is a plain file download. Nothing about you, your files or your library is sent. As with any web request, Hugging Face and its content network see your IP address and the request itself, under their own privacy policy. Once a pack is on your PC, the feature it powers runs offline. You can remove any pack in Settings → On-device packs.

You can skip packs entirely. Tidying, finding copies, renaming, cleaning up and undo all work without them.

3.2 Looking up place names (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Settings → Look up place names online, typing a place name on the map sends only the words you typed to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service (nominatim.openstreetmap.org), which turns them into coordinates. The request carries a standard app identifier ("Sortwise") in its User-Agent header.

Sortwise never sends your file names, your photos, the locations saved in your photos, your index or anything else from your library.

The setting is off until you turn it on. With it off, or with no network at all, Sortwise still places your photos on the map from the GPS data already inside them, entirely offline.

Nominatim is run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation, and their privacy policy covers your request.

4. Face data

If you turn on Find the people in photos, Sortwise finds faces in your photos and makes face templates (numbers) so it can group photos of the same person.

  • Finding and matching faces runs entirely on your PC.
  • Face templates and face crops are kept only in the local database and cache described in section 2.
  • Face data is never uploaded, never shared, never used for advertising and never used to identify anyone outside your own photos. Sortwise does not know who anyone is. It only groups similar faces until you choose to name a group.
  • Face data is deliberately kept out of cloud-synced locations.
  • The problem log never contains face data.
  • You can delete all of it at any time in Settings → Delete all face data. This permanently erases every face, group and name and the cached face crops, compacts the database file so the deleted data is not left inside it, and turns finding people off.

Sortwise looks only at photos in the places you gave it, and only after you turn the feature on.

5. On-device AI

Sortwise's AI features (searching photos by what is in them, spotting look-alikes, grouping photos by face, and turning your own words into rules) run entirely on your PC, using the packs stored in the folder described in section 2. No prompt, image, embedding or file name is sent anywhere to be processed. There is no AI service behind this app.

Sortwise does not use the AI models built into Windows, and it sends nothing to Microsoft for processing.

6. Buying Pro, rating and sharing

Pro add-ons. Sortwise is free. The optional add-ons ("Pro for 24 hours" and "Pro forever") are sold through the Microsoft Store. The Store, not Sortwise, handles the payment, and Microsoft's privacy statement (privacy.microsoft.com) covers it. We never see your payment details, your name or your Microsoft account. Sortwise only asks the Store whether this PC has the add-on. The count of free file changes you have used is kept on your PC (section 1) and is never sent anywhere.

Rating. Rate Sortwise opens the Microsoft Store's own rating window. Anything you write there goes to Microsoft under their terms, not to the app.

Telling a friend. Refer a friend opens the Windows share panel with a short message and the Sortwise Store link. It sends nothing itself. Only the app you choose in the share panel receives that message, and only if you send it.

7. Notifications

If you turn on Settings → Tell me when new files arrive, Sortwise shows a Windows notification about new files in watched folders, at most once a week. The notification is made on your PC and sent to no one.

8. Problem log

Sortwise writes problem and crash details to a plain-text file in the logs folder on your PC. This log is never sent anywhere. There is no crash-reporting service, no telemetry and no usage analytics in this app. The log is yours: you can open it from Settings → Problem log, attach it to an email to us if you choose, or delete it.

9. Permissions Sortwise asks for

  • Full file system access (runFullTrust): so Sortwise can read, move, rename and recycle files in the places you pick, including places outside its own sandbox. Sortwise changes files only in your places. If you turn on Measure whole drives, the Disk view also adds up the sizes of other folders on a drive, but nothing outside your places is ever changed.

10. Your control over your data

  • Forget what Sortwise has read: Settings → Forget what Sortwise has read clears the index. Your files, places and undo history are kept.
  • Forget a place: Settings → Places Sortwise knows removes a place from the index without touching its files.
  • Turn features off: reading document text, reading photo dates and music tags, finding people, online place lookup, background reading and notifications each have their own switch in Settings.
  • Delete face data: as described in section 4.
  • Remove a pack: Settings → On-device packs.
  • Delete everything: uninstall Sortwise and delete the %LOCALAPPDATA%\Sortwise folder. That removes the index, the packs and the log. Your own photos and documents are not touched, because they are yours and Sortwise never deletes them behind your back.
  • Undo: every change Sortwise makes is recorded on your PC so you can reverse it from the Timeline. Deleted files go to the Windows Recycle Bin, and Sortwise refuses to delete where there is no Recycle Bin to catch them.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete for you and no account for you to close.

11. Children

Sortwise is a general-purpose file tool. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them, or from anyone else.

12. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your data, because we never have it. The app contains no third-party advertising, tracking or profiling code.
