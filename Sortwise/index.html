Every feature

Everything Sortwise does, in detail

ORGANIZE FILES AND PHOTOS

  • One-click recipes: by file type, by year and month, by year, by type then year, by camera, or move screenshots aside.
  • Continue the filing system you already have. Sortwise reads the folders you built years ago and writes rules that carry on where you left off.
  • Describe what you want in your own words — "put invoices in Documents by year" — and a local language model drafts the rules for you to check.
  • A full rule builder: extension, name, path, size, dates, image size, camera, person, place, label, visual similarity.
  • Save any set of rules as a recipe and run it again whenever you like.
  • Watched folders notice new arrivals and stage them for review. They never move anything on their own.
  • Files that belong together stay together — a RAW and its JPG, a video and its sidecar, are never split up.
  • Protected system locations are refused outright.

BATCH RENAME

  • Replace text, number files in sequence, add a prefix or a suffix, fix capitalisation, or strip text out — across hundreds of files at once, with the same preview and the same one-click undo.

FIND ANYTHING AGAIN

  • Search files by name, type, size, date or your own labels.
  • Search photos by what is actually in them — "beach", "my dog", "receipts" — even when every file is called IMG_4471.
  • Search inside your documents, including PDFs.

PHOTOS

  • People: Sortwise groups photos of the same face so you can put a name to a group and file pictures by person. Off by default, opt-in, and erasable at any time. It never guesses who anyone is.
  • Places: see where your photos were taken, grouped into trips and locations, using the position your camera or phone stored in the picture.
  • Bursts: twenty-four frames of the same moment, with the sharpest one picked out for you.

DUPLICATES AND DISK SPACE

  • Identical files anywhere on your drives, whatever they happen to be called.
  • Near-identical photos, caught even after they have been resized, re-compressed or shared around.
  • Debris: interrupted downloads, editor temp files, empty files, old installers, and archives already unpacked beside them.
  • Archive a folder you have not touched in months into a .zip beside it, checked file by file before anything is removed.

PAPERWORK

  • Bills, statements and scans arrive as things like Statement_08472204_20240314.pdf. Sortwise reads the text already stored for search and suggests who sent it, what kind of document it is and when — enough to rename it "2024-03-14 - Barclays - Statement.pdf" and file it by year. The evidence is shown next to every suggestion, and it goes through the same preview as everything else.

BROWSE, BACK UP, REPAIR

  • A built-in folder browser: work through your library folder by folder, sort by size, and move, rename, label or recycle what you find without leaving the app.
  • Move a large folder to another drive and leave a link behind, so shortcuts, projects and anything else pointing at the old place keeps working.
  • Incremental backup jobs between your folders, with restore and verification.
  • Compare two folders and see exactly what is missing, changed or duplicated between them.
  • Fix names that break things: paths over 260 characters, trailing spaces, characters cloud storage refuses.
  • Full history: every change Sortwise has ever made to any file — moved, copied, recycled, unpacked, renamed — newest first, and every one of them undoable.
Private by design

Your files stay on your PC

Sortwise has no server to send anything to. What it learns about your files is kept in a local database on your own drive.

Sortwise works offline. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no telemetry. Your files, your photos, your face groupings and your searches stay on your machine — the index is a local database on your own drive and is never uploaded.

Two things use the internet, both optional and both only when you ask:

  • A one-time download of the AI model files Sortwise then runs on your own hardware.
  • Place-name lookup via OpenStreetMap, which is off by default and sends only the words you type.

Face data never leaves your PC, is kept out of cloud-synced folders, and can be erased completely from Settings at any time. If you would rather Sortwise never looked inside a file at all, one setting restricts it to file names and locations only.
